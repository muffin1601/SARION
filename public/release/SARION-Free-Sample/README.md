## ⚡ Quick Start

1. **Read** [`Quick-Start-Guide.md`](Quick-Start-Guide.md) — get productive in 10 minutes.
2. **Browse** [`01-Prompts/`](01-Prompts/) — find a prompt for your task and paste it into Claude Code.
3. **Follow** a playbook in [`02-Debugging/`](02-Debugging/) or [`09-SOPs/`](09-SOPs/) when you hit a wall.
4. **Copy** a template from [`06-PRDs/`](06-PRDs/) or [`07-Readme-Templates/`](07-Readme-Templates/) to start any project.

---

## 💡 How To Use This Kit

- **Prompts** are copy-paste ready. Replace `{{PLACEHOLDERS}}` with your context.
- **Playbooks** are step-by-step. Follow them top to bottom under pressure.
- **Templates** are starting points. Fork, fill, ship.
- **Checklists** are pre-flight. Run them before every launch.
