# SEO authority-building plan

## P1 assets

| Asset | Target cluster | Audience | Outreach targets | Rationale | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agency operations scorecard | Agency management and operations | Agency founders | Agency newsletters, communities, consultants | A diagnostic is useful before a software evaluation and can earn contextual mentions. | P1 |
| Project profitability calculator | Project management and invoicing | Operations leads | Finance/agency operations publications | A practical calculator creates a citation-worthy decision tool. | P1 |
| Client onboarding checklist | CRM, client management and portals | Account managers | Agency educators and template libraries | A reusable checklist supports delivery workflows and natural links. | P1 |
| Agency CRM selection guide | Agency CRM | Small-agency buyers | SaaS comparison writers and fractional COOs | Publish decision criteria, not unsupported competitor claims. | P1 |
| Client portal rollout template | Client portal | Client-services teams | Design and client-experience communities | Helps agencies implement a portal rather than merely evaluate one. | P2 |

Do not buy links, automate outreach, or make claims that cannot be substantiated.
